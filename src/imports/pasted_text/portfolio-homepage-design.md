DESIGN PRINCIPLES
Throughout the design:
- prioritize white space
- allow project imagery to breathe
- use strong visual hierarchy
- avoid making every section the same height or structure
- create intentional rhythm as the user scrolls
- alternate pale striped, deep navy, ivory, and pale blue sections
- use subtle 1px rules and restrained borders
- use very mild shadows only when necessary
- keep corners relatively subtle, around 2–8px rather than highly rounded cards
- use asymmetry carefully
- create a custom editorial feel
- make the page look expensive and thoughtfully art-directed
- maintain strong alignment despite the editorial layout

The final result should feel like a premium portfolio for a corporate marketing and digital strategy professional, not a graphic-design student portfolio.
